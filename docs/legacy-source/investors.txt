PROPOSED COMMERCIAL MODEL
## Match the revenue model
 to the product.
 Proposed adoption paths. No booked revenue, signed customer contract or validated price is claimed.
  | Offering  | Potential buyer  | Paid entry / expansion
  | Battery & Charging AI  | Battery/BMS supplier or OEM  | Paid integration/PoC → embedded-runtime licensing and program support.
  | PMG embedded software  | OEM, vehicle platform or fleet program  | Scoped vehicle workflow → program or per-vehicle licensing, subject to agreed deployment terms.
  | PMG cloud services  | OEM quality/service organization or fleet  | Authorized evidence workflow → enterprise subscription/services where recurring value is proven.
  | Evo Core  | OEM or EV power-system partner  | Paid engineering/reference program → hardware supply and embedded-software/support terms.
  | Engineering tools  | Engineering and validation teams  | Focused workflow pilot → enterprise tooling/services, as an enabling track.
{id=entry}
A DISCIPLINED MARKET ENTRY
## Choose the buyer.
 Prove the first wedge.
 01
### A costly, observable problem
Agree a charging-readiness, health or investigation case with an accountable engineering or service owner.
 02
### An existing baseline
Measure current detection, readiness, investigation effort and the complete cost of the issue.
 03
### A bounded implementation
Use approved data and controlled testing before any permitted vehicle action or wider deployment.
 04
### A paid next step
Seek acceptance against the agreed gate and validate integration responsibility, pricing and willingness to adopt.
 ECONOMICS TO VALIDATE
### Net value includes the whole adoption cost.
Attributable issue cost avoided + engineering/service time saved + measured energy value − integration, runtime, validation, operation and support cost. Avoid double-counting the same benefit across products.
### The numbers the next proof must establish
Buyer interviews, addressable reference programs, pricing, unit economics, TAM/SAM/SOM and the funding ask require a sourced, bottom-up case. They remain open rather than being presented as traction.
{id=milestones}
CAPITAL ALIGNED TO EVIDENCE
## Fund the next milestone.
 Learn before scaling.
A proposed 12–16 week PMG software PoC starts after partner, approved data and test access are agreed. Battery AI model development and Evo Core hardware require separately scoped work packages.
 WEEKS 0–2
### Define the baseline
Problem, buyer, signal access, permissions and an agreed acceptance gate.
 WEEKS 2–6
### Connect the runtime
Read-only data path, vehicle context, policy checks and a reviewable bench integration.
 WEEKS 6–10
### Evaluate the workflow
Replay and controlled tests, false alerts, model/runtime needs and traceable outcomes.
 WEEKS 10–16
### Decide the next program
Incremental value, complete costs and a buyer-led discussion about paid expansion.
 SOFTWARE / DATA
### Runtime and evaluation
Model work, signal integration, edge runtime, versioned evidence and controlled tests.
 HARDWARE / VALIDATION
### Reference and bench proof
Supplier-interface access, control feasibility, prototype planning and bottom-up cost evidence.
 BUYER / ADOPTION
### Commercial proof
Partner discovery, acceptance criteria, pricing and a clear ownership/support model.
