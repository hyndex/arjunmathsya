A CLEAR ROLE FOR EACH PRODUCT
## Hardware, embedded AI
 and vehicle software.
A customer can begin with one product. The connected architecture is the expansion path, rather than a requirement to purchase the complete portfolio.
 Proposed product roles and commercial paths; production specifications and pricing are not released.
  | Product  | What it does / where it runs  | Who could adopt / commercial path
  | Battery & Charging AI  | Embedded software on suitable BMS or adjacent module compute; health trends, state-estimation evaluation and charging context.  | Battery and BMS suppliers, OEMs; scoped engineering and embedded-runtime licensing.
  | Evo Core  | EV power hardware plus embedded software; shared control, defined power functions and AI supervision.  | OEMs and power-system partners; paid development, supply and program software/support options.

  | PMG cloud services  | Optional off-vehicle software; authorized fleet evidence, quality workflows and service learning.  | OEM or fleet organizations; SaaS subscription or enterprise terms after the value case is proven.
{id=interfaces}
HOW INTEGRATION WORKS
## Connect through interfaces.
 Keep ownership clear.
 01
### Observe the module
Use approved signals and diagnostics from existing sensing and control. Define what is available, at what quality and for which operating conditions.
 02
### Evaluate on the edge
Run a bounded model on suitable compute. Retain confidence, version and evidence; compare its usefulness with deterministic and model-based methods.
 03
### Coordinate with PMG
Exchange health and context events through the approved vehicle network. PMG combines module information with journey, customer and service intent.
 04
### Verify and learn
Request only permitted actions. Check outcomes locally and share authorized, relevant evidence with quality or fleet services.
 The deployment architecture follows the vehicle.
The exact network, APIs, compute, memory, latency and diagnostic integration are set during the reference program. Data access and secure update responsibilities need an agreed owner.

{id=principles}
ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES
## Useful AI.
 Defined authority.
 LOCAL OPERATION
### Edge inference first.
Evaluate selected functions within the vehicle. Optional cloud learning adds value when available; connectivity loss has a defined local behavior.
 VALIDATED CONTROL
### Respect existing limits.
Protection, real-time control and permitted actuator authority belong to the validated vehicle architecture. Model output is not an unrestricted control command.
 EVIDENCE & ADOPTION
### Prove incremental value.
Measure the improvement over existing methods, including false alerts, resource needs, integration, validation and support costs.
BUILD THE FIRST PROOF
