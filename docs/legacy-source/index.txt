01 / THE OPPORTUNITY
## A vehicle has signals.
 Value needs context.
A battery knows its operating state. A charger knows its session. A power module knows its temperature. Mathsya’s direction is to interpret those signals locally, connect them to the vehicle’s needs and preserve evidence that helps engineering and service.
 THE CUSTOMER
### Ready for the next journey.
Useful charging guidance, understandable health information and better-informed service planning.
 THE MODULE
### Understand changing behavior.
Evaluate trends across usage and conditions, then compare the insight with today’s estimation and diagnostic methods.
 THE OEM
### Turn events into learning.
Link evidence to quality, warranty and product decisions; verify whether the correction actually helped.

{id=benefit-number}01 / BATTERY CARE
### {id=benefit-title}Understand the battery behind the percentage.
{id=benefit-scene}A driver sees a charge percentage. The useful question is whether the battery is ready for the next journey, and whether its behavior is changing.
 THE INPUT
{id=benefit-signals}Available cell voltages, pack current, temperatures, charging history and validated battery-state estimates.
 01
 MODULE INTELLIGENCE
{id=benefit-module}Battery AI evaluates health trends and unusual behavior, with confidence and data-quality checks.
 02
 PMG / VEHICLE CONTEXT
{id=benefit-pmg}PMG adds departure, journey and service context, then prepares a permitted recommendation and checks its outcome.
 FOR THE CUSTOMER
{id=benefit-customer}Clearer battery-health guidance and a charging or service decision that fits the next journey.
 FOR THE OEM
{id=benefit-oem}Evidence to investigate degradation patterns, review service decisions and identify repeat issues.
 WHAT TO MEASURE
{id=benefit-proof}Held-out state-estimation error, actionable alerts, false alerts, customer overrides and independently verified health outcomes.
Illustrative workflows. Outcomes depend on data access, integration and evaluation against the existing system; no achieved savings or battery-life gains are claimed.
