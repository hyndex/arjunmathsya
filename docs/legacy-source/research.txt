01 / PRODUCT NEURAL CHAIN
## Engineering decisions
with connected memory.
 Concept & PoC definition
An evidence-linked approach to product lifecycle decisions: connect requirements, architecture, cost, reuse, validation and field feedback. Recommendations remain reviewable by the engineering owners.
 01Requirements & scope
 02Architecture & cost
 03Validation & release
 04Field learning
### A focused pilot
One product family and a small set of lifecycle decisions, with known source documents and decision owners.
### The value to establish
Decision time, reusable evidence, confirmed reuse opportunities and the cost impact of accepted changes.
 Proof gate
Traceable recommendations and verified changes must support a better engineering decision than the existing workflow.

02 / ENGINEERING INTELLIGENCE
## From a release event
to a clearer investigation.
 Workflow & validation design
Link release changes, connected-service tests and system logs to localize failures and compare behavior across releases. The proposed initial scope includes remote operations, cellular connectivity and GNSS.
EVIDENCE FOR RELEASE & QUALITY TEAMS
 Change and test evidence Release context, test results and relevant logs
→
 Reviewable investigation Compare behavior and test a likely explanation
→
 Verified correction Check the fix and preserve reusable evidence
### A focused pilot
Select a connected function and representative failures. Compare assisted investigations with the current process.
### The value to establish
Failure-localization time, evidence completeness and behavior after an approved correction.

03 / SOLAR-ASSISTED AUXILIARIES
## Use available sunlight.
 Measure useful energy.
 Research & energy modeling
Explore modest vehicle-roof solar supplying a defined low-voltage auxiliary load, with conventional vehicle power providing backup. This is a separate energy and commercial research track.
TECHNICAL QUESTION
### Does usable energy meet the load?
Evaluate sunny, cloudy and shaded parking, usable storage, conversion losses and the actual auxiliary demand.
COMMERCIAL QUESTION
### Does the installation earn its cost?
Include integration, storage, repair and lifetime performance alongside the reduction in energy supplied by the vehicle.
 Research boundary
Auxiliary energy coverage alone does not establish attractive payback. A credible prototype should compare measured useful energy and the complete installed cost.
## Choose one engineering question.
 Agree on the evidence.
Supporting programs begin with a named decision, baseline and owner. Pilot scope follows the strongest technical and business case.
