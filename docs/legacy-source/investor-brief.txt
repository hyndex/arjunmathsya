## The problem and direction
Module signals become more valuable when interpreted in context and linked to a useful decision. Mathsya’s direction starts with one battery, charging or power-system problem, connects it to vehicle and customer needs, and preserves evidence for service and OEM quality. Existing estimation, diagnostics and control form the baseline.
## The product portfolio
MODULE SOFTWARE
### Battery & Charging AI
Embedded intelligence for health/state-estimation evaluation, charging readiness, thermal context and available cell-level patterns, alongside the validated BMS.
Proposed adoption: paid integration and embedded-runtime licensing.
VEHICLE SOFTWARE / OPTIONAL SaaS
### PMG
Proactive Mobility Guardian joins selected module insight with journey and customer context, permitted recommendations and verified outcomes. Optional cloud services extend authorized OEM/fleet learning.
Proposed adoption: vehicle-software licensing and optional enterprise/cloud services.
POWER HARDWARE + SOFTWARE
### Evo Core
A coordinated EV power platform with common control and AI supervision. The proposed roadmap progresses from charging/power to battery/thermal and wider energy coordination.
Proposed adoption: paid engineering, hardware supply and program software/support.
## The Evo Core generation roadmap
### Gen 1 → Gen 2 → Gen 3
Gen 1: OBC, DC/DC, EVCC, HV distribution and common control. Gen 2: battery-state context, AI and broader thermal/charging options. Gen 3: wider energy supervision and optional inverter coordination.
### Integration follows evidence
Common control, physical power-stage integration and connected interfaces are distinct decisions. BMS sensing/protection, real-time controls and qualification retain defined ownership. Generations have no committed release dates.
## Benefits and proof
### Customer outcomes
Useful readiness and charging guidance, clearer battery/thermal context and better-informed service planning. Measure verified outcomes, false alerts and customer overrides.
### OEM outcomes
Evidence-led investigations, repeat-issue learning and a credible commonization cost case. Measure investigation time, recurrence, reusable software and complete program cost.
No achieved battery-life, range, warranty or savings percentages are claimed. Compare with existing methods and include data, integration, compute, validation and support costs.
## The next milestone
- Agree one problem, partner, approved data, baseline and acceptance gate.
- For a proposed 12–16 week PMG software PoC, build a reviewable integration and evaluate a controlled workflow after access is agreed.
- Scope Battery AI model development and Evo Core hardware as separate work packages with their own evidence gates.
- Validate buyer willingness to adopt, pricing and a costed paid follow-on program.
## Founder and investment purpose
The founder brings an automotive engineering background spanning vehicle programs, connected services, telematics hardware, validation and EV power electronics. Funding should align to agreed software/data, hardware/bench and buyer/adoption work packages. The funding amount, market sizing and commercial terms require a sourced, bottom-up plan.
Development-stage public overview. Architecture, generation scopes, benefits and commercial models are proposed. Production qualification, customer contracts and measured benefit claims are not established in this brief.
