# Mathsya Connected Services — Investor & Partner Brief
Automotive Edge AI from module behaviour to customer and OEM value
October 2026 | Founder: Arjun Amuthan · Bengaluru, India
Current stage: Architecture, use cases and proof of concept planning

## The opportunity
Battery, charging and power-system signals can support more useful decisions when they are interpreted in context and linked to a verified outcome. Mathsya starts with one observable customer or engineering problem and an existing workflow against which to measure value.

## The products
- **Battery & Charging AI:** Proposed embedded software for health trends, charging readiness, thermal context and accessible cell patterns, alongside a validated BMS. Potential commercial path: paid integration, embedded-runtime licensing and program support.
- **PMG:** Proactive Mobility Guardian connects selected module insights with vehicle, journey and customer context. It prepares permitted recommendations and checks their outcomes. Optional cloud services extend authorized OEM and fleet learning. Potential commercial path: embedded licensing and optional enterprise services.
- **Evo Core:** A proposed EV power platform combining common control, defined power conversion and distribution functions, and supervisory intelligence. Gen 1 establishes charging and power functions; Gen 2 explores battery and thermal extensions; Gen 3 explores wider energy coordination and optional inverter supervision. Hardware scopes and release dates depend on reference-program evidence.

## Benefits to evaluate
Useful charging readiness, clearer health or thermal guidance and informed service planning for customers. Focused investigation evidence, repeat-issue learning, reusable integration and a complete cost case for OEMs and partners.

## The first proof
Agree one problem, a buyer, approved data, a baseline and an acceptance gate. A proposed 12–16 week PMG software PoC begins after access is agreed. Battery model development and Evo Core hardware require separately scoped work packages. Compare useful outcomes, error, false alerts, runtime needs and total adoption cost.

## The partnership sought
An accountable OEM, BMS, power-system or fleet problem owner; approved signal and test access; and milestone-based resources or funding for a measurable first application. Pricing, market size, funding amount and commercial terms require an agreed bottom-up plan.

## Founder background
Arjun Amuthan brings automotive engineering experience across vehicle programs, connected services, telematics hardware, validation and EV power electronics.
