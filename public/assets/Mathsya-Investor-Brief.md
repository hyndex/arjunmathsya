# Mathsya Connected Services — Investor & Partner Brief
October 2026 | Founder: Arjun Amuthan | Bengaluru, India

## Direction
Automotive Edge AI connecting module behavior to customer and OEM outcomes. Current stage: architecture, use-case definition and proof-of-concept planning.

## Product portfolio
- **Battery & Charging AI:** module software for health/state-estimation evaluation, charging readiness, thermal context and accessible cell-level patterns, alongside a validated BMS. Proposed model: integration and embedded-runtime licensing.
- **PMG — Proactive Mobility Guardian:** vehicle software joining selected module insights with vehicle, journey and customer context, permitted recommendations and verified outcomes. Optional cloud/SaaS services support authorized OEM/fleet learning. Proposed model: embedded licensing and optional enterprise/cloud services.
- **Evo Core:** EV power hardware plus embedded software, common control and Edge AI supervision. Proposed model: engineering, hardware supply and program software/support.

## Proposed Evo Core roadmap
Gen 1: OBC, DC/DC, EVCC, HV distribution and common control.
Gen 2: battery-state context, Battery & Charging AI and broader thermal coordination; DC-session and bidirectional functions are options needing compatible hardware and validation.
Gen 3: wider energy supervision and optional inverter coordination, subject to compute, isolation, thermal and qualification feasibility.
Generation scopes are a working roadmap; no release dates or production specifications are committed. Shared control, power-stage integration and connected interfaces are separate decisions. Cell sensing/protection and validated real-time control keep defined ownership.

## Benefits to validate
Customer: readiness, useful charging/health guidance and informed service planning.
OEM: investigation evidence, repeat-issue learning, reusable integration and a complete commonization cost case.
Compare with existing methods. Measure independent outcomes, errors, false alerts, overrides, resources and full adoption cost. Do not claim achieved battery-life, range or savings percentages.

## First proof and investment
One partner-defined problem, approved data, a baseline and an acceptance gate. A proposed 12–16 week PMG software PoC begins after partner/data/test access is agreed. Battery AI model work and Evo Core hardware need separately scoped development and qualification gates.
Funding purpose: software/data integration, hardware/bench evidence and buyer/adoption validation. Funding amount, pricing, TAM/SAM/SOM and unit economics require a sourced, bottom-up plan; they are not established traction.
