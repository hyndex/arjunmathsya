# Mathsya — Proactive Mobility Guardian (PMG)
October 2026 | Vehicle software with optional cloud services | Architecture and PoC planning

## Connect the insight. Make it useful.
Proactive Mobility Guardian is Mathsya's vehicle software direction. PMG connects selected module insights with vehicle, journey and customer context, prepares permitted recommendations and verifies the outcome. It can start with existing module data without replacing every module.

## On the vehicle
PMG can run on suitable gateway, telematics or central vehicle compute selected for the reference architecture. It combines approved inputs, retains focused operating context and defines local behaviour for missing data or lost connectivity. Exact APIs, network, memory, latency and compute are established in the integration proof.

## Optional cloud services
With agreed access and permissions, selected evidence can support OEM quality investigations, service workflows, fleet comparisons and tracking of a correction. These services can have a SaaS or enterprise delivery model; they are separate from the embedded runtime.

## Worked example (illustrative): a warm battery, an early departure, a useful plan
A vehicle arrives after a long drive with a warm battery, plugs in overnight and has a departure planned for 07:30. Battery & Charging AI supplies available condition and confidence information. PMG adds departure intent, charging status and customer permissions. It evaluates guidance or an approved scheduling or preconditioning request, checks the outcome and retains relevant evidence. This is an illustrative workflow for evaluation.

Two further candidate workflows: OEM quality (a local behaviour change linked to vehicle and version context, then verification of an approved correction) and incident context (an existing authoritative incident trigger, preserved before-and-after context, a permitted handoff and acknowledgement).

## Value to evaluate
Customer: charging guidance that fits the next journey, understandable recommendations and better-informed service planning.
OEM: events linked to operating context and hardware or software versions, comparable investigation evidence and verification after an approved correction.

## First proof
A proposed 12–16 week PMG software PoC begins after partner, approved data and test access are agreed. Define the baseline (weeks 0–2), connect the runtime (2–6), evaluate a controlled workflow (6–10) and decide the next paid program (10–16). Battery model development and Evo Core hardware require separately scoped work.

## Authority
Validated control, protection and safety functions retain authority. Available interfaces and customer permissions determine what PMG may recommend or request.

## Commercial direction
Program integration, embedded-software licensing and support, with optional enterprise subscriptions or services where recurring organizational value is demonstrated.
